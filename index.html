Privacy Policy for Poker Preflop

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Introduction

Poker Preflop is a mobile app developed by Pavel Gomarov. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data We Collect

Poker Preflop does not collect personal data on our servers.

Information you create in the app (preflop charts and your edits, imported chart packs, and settings such as appearance, haptics, anonymous analytics, and the last table spot) is stored locally on your device.

The app also sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, as described below. Chart contents, pack names, and author names are not included. The choice to allow or refuse these statistics is stored on your device.

Poker Preflop does not require account registration and does not provide cloud sync in the current version.

Purchases

Poker Preflop offers auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time Lifetime purchase, both processed by Apple through the App Store. A subscription renews until you cancel it. Lifetime is a single payment that unlocks Premium without a recurring charge.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Billing and payment data are handled by Apple.

We may receive limited purchase status information from Apple (for example, whether a subscription is active or whether Lifetime has been purchased) to unlock premium features.

Import, Export, and Support

If you export a chart pack or share the app, that file or link is shared only through the system share sheet you choose. We do not upload chart packs to a server.

If you contact support from the app, the draft email includes the app version, build number, iOS version, and device model. That information is sent only if you send the email.

Third-Party Services

Poker Preflop does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). The app does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Poker Preflop uses TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used, for example which screens are opened and whether a subscription or the Lifetime purchase is completed. TelemetryDeck is not used for advertising or for cross-app tracking.

Before anything is sent, TelemetryDeck turns a random identifier created on the device into a hash. The identifier itself does not leave the device and is not tied to your name, email, or Apple ID. Signals can also include technical details such as app version, iOS version, device model, language, and coarse time zone, plus the usage events described above.

These statistics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Anonymous Analytics. After you turn them off, the app stops sending new events. Signals already received by TelemetryDeck cannot be tied back to you.

TelemetryDeck’s own policy is at https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/.

Purchases and the App Store page are provided by Apple.

Children’s Privacy

Poker Preflop is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Your Choices

Because app content is stored locally, you can edit or replace charts in the app, or remove them by deleting the app (subject to iOS backup behavior).

You can turn anonymous usage statistics off or on in Settings → Anonymous Analytics. The app does not require an account, so there is no analytics profile to delete on our side.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information (such as access, deletion, or correction).

If you have a privacy request, contact us and we will do our best to assist based on the data we actually process.
