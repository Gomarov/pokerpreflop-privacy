Privacy Policy

Poker Preflop (“the app”) is a preflop chart reference. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device.

Charts stay on your device

Built-in charts, your edits, imported charts, and the daily usage counter are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not upload them to a server operated by the developer.

No tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not collect personal data and does not sell or share data with third parties.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
