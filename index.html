Privacy Policy for Poker Preflop

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Introduction

Poker Preflop is a mobile app developed by Pavel Gomarov. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data We Collect

Poker Preflop does not collect personal data on our servers.

Information you create in the app (preflop charts and your edits, imported chart packs, the daily usage counter, and settings such as appearance, haptics, and the last table spot) is stored locally on your device.

Poker Preflop does not require account registration and does not provide cloud sync in the current version.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Poker Preflop offers auto-renewable subscriptions processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Billing and payment data are handled by Apple.

We may receive limited purchase status information from Apple (for example, whether a subscription is active) to unlock premium features.

Import, Export, and Support

If you export a chart pack or share the app, that file or link is shared only through the system share sheet you choose. We do not upload chart packs to a server.

If you contact support from the app, the draft email includes the app version, build number, iOS version, and device model. That information is sent only if you send the email.

Third-Party Services

Poker Preflop does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Poker Preflop does not use third-party analytics SDKs in the current version.

Purchases and the App Store page are provided by Apple. If advertising or analytics are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy and App Store privacy details will be updated before release.

Children’s Privacy

Poker Preflop is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Your Choices

Because app content is stored locally, you can edit or replace charts in the app, or remove them by deleting the app (subject to iOS backup behavior).

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information (such as access, deletion, or correction).

If you have a privacy request, contact us and we will do our best to assist based on the data we actually process.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, please contact us at:

  • Email: pokerpreflop@protonmail.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and update the “Effective Date” above.

Thank you for using Poker Preflop!
